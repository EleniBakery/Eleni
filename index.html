Meet the chef

Hi, I'm Eleni

I'm a Ukrainian pastry chef living in Nicosia. I fell in love with French patisserie: its textures, its balance and its attention to every detail. Now I bring that to Cyprus, together with the cakes I grew up with.

Every order is made by me, from start to finish, in small batches. I use real butter, fine chocolate and fresh fruit, and no shortcuts. I'd love to make your celebration a little sweeter.

Good to know

Questions

/01How far in advance should I order?

Ideally 5–7 days ahead for most cakes, and 2–4 weeks for weddings and large events. Last-minute? Message me and I'll check what's possible.

/02Do you deliver?

Pick-up is in Nicosia. Delivery within Nicosia can be arranged for an extra fee. Just ask when you order.

/03Can you make a custom design?

Of course! Send me your theme, colours or an inspiration photo, and I'll suggest a design and flavours.

/04Do you cater for allergies?

Please tell me about any allergies when you order. Note that all desserts are made in a kitchen that handles nuts, gluten, dairy and eggs.

/05How do I pay?

A deposit confirms your order, and the rest is paid on pick-up or delivery. I'll share the details when we confirm.

/06How should I store my cake?

Keep mousse cakes refrigerated and take them out about 20–30 minutes before serving for the best texture.
